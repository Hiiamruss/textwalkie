How to Chat with others?

Step 1. Enter a code, it could be a random word, a memory, or a number. Any will do.

Step 2. Give your friend the code and make them join. Then whatever message is in that room will be shown to whoever is joining.

Disclaimer: The chatrooms are not private, anyone can access the chats at anytime, anywhere.

Joining Hubs

Hubs are public socializing places, join the neohub by typing "neohub.cc" in the code field when joining.
